📌 What is Google STEP?

STEP stands for Student Training in Engineering Program. It is Google's internship program designed specifically for 1st-year and 2nd-year undergraduate students in Computer Science and related fields.

Google created STEP because they believe in investing early — finding talented students before the traditional internship cycle and giving them a head start in their engineering careers.

⏱️ Duration~10–12 weeks (summer internship)
📍 LocationGoogle offices (Bangalore, Hyderabad for India)
💰 StipendHighly competitive (among the highest freshman internships globally)

💡 5 Golden Interview Tips

  • Think out loud: Share your thoughts before typing any code. Interviewers want to see how you think.
  • Start with brute force: State the obvious solution first, then analyze and optimize.
  • Test your code manually: Trace a small example on paper or in comments before saying "I'm done".
  • Ask clarifying questions: "Can the input be empty?" "Are numbers negative?" Shows engineering maturity.
  • Be honest and calm: If stuck, say "I'm reasoning through this..." and break down what you know.
